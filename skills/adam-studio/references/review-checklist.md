# Before showing a version

Look with `still` at frame 1, the hook (0 to 3 s), the boldest moment of each variant and the ending.

- Frame 1 already shows the hook's subject or motion: never black, blank, or a title over nothing.
- The hook lands within 3 seconds; its on-screen text complements the first spoken words, it does not repeat them.
- The face stays clear: nothing over the eyes or mouth; the speaker on screen at least 65 % of the time.
- Text inside the safe area (x 72 to 900, y 269 to 1248 on 1080x1920), readable at phone size; one emphasis mechanism.
- Captions: exact words, one or two lines, no orphan word, held long enough to read.
- Pace: hard cuts by default, a transition only at a section change; at most 3 inserts per minute.
- Inserts all in the look's treatment (same black and white, grain, bloom); the presenter keeps their own grade.
- Sound: speech first; effects on graphics, none on bare cuts or on a word.
- The ending gives the viewer something: a tip, a takeaway, a next step.
- Duration, format and the creator's approved text and audio unchanged unless that was the point.

Report what you checked by looking, and what you could not check (sound cannot be judged from stills).
